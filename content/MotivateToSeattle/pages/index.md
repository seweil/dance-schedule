# Motivate to Seattle

The 2026 West Coast A & C Weekend — October 9–11, 2026, at the Hilton
Seattle Airport & Conference Center in Seattle, Washington, hosted by
Puddletown Squares.

A weekend of Advanced and Challenge square dancing, starting Friday evening
with GCA caller slots and a trail-in dance, and running through Sunday
afternoon.

- [**Event Schedule**](/event-schedule) — meals, registration, the trail-in
  dance, and everything else on the calendar that isn't a dance session.
- [**Dance Schedule**](/dance-schedule) — the full dance session grid, room
  by room.
- [**Dance by Level**](/dance-by-level) — the same sessions, organized by
  skill level instead.
- [**Callers**](/callers) — who's calling this weekend.

New here? See [Installation](/installation) to add this schedule to your
phone's home screen — handy for checking between tips if the hotel's Wi-Fi
is spotty.

## Registration

- Full registration: $225/person (includes Sunday brunch)
- Day rates: Friday $45, Saturday morning $50 / afternoon $60 / evening $50,
  Sunday $45 (brunch not included)
- Non-dancer: brunch only, $40

Register through the [IAGSDC website](https://iagsdc.org/event/798), or by
[mail-in form](https://puddletownsquares.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/motivate-to-seattle-registration.pdf)
(check payable to Puddletown Squares) or
[PayPal](https://www.paypal.com/ncp/payment/Z3PJVA3EF4MM2).

## Hotel

Hilton Seattle Airport & Conference Center, 17620 International Blvd,
Seattle, WA 98188. Rooms from $169/night (King, Queen, or Single/Double) —
[book through the hotel's block](https://book.passkey.com/go/Puddletown26).

See you on the floor!

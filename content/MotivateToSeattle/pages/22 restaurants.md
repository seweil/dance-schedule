# Restaurant Guide — Near Hilton Seattle Airport & Conference Center

Restaurants are grouped by distance from the hotel and by price/formality. Ratings and review counts are from Google (pulled Aug 2026) and summarized by AI.

> **Note:** Google also lists many nearby restaurants that are inside security at the airport. Check before you go.

---

## Walking Distance (under 10 min on foot)

### [Spencer's](https://www.hilton.com/en/hotels/seaahhh-hilton-seattle-airport-and-conference-center/dining/)
Right here in the Hilton.

### [13 Coins SeaTac](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=13+Coins+SeaTac+18000+International+Blvd+SeaTac+WA)
18000 International Blvd — ~0.2 mi, inside the DoubleTree next door
**4.3★ (8,043 reviews)** · $$ · Open until 11 PM (1 AM Fri/Sat)
American/Italian. The French onion soup comes up unprompted in review after review. Reliable for a group booth-and-bar dinner.

### [Reflections Bar & Grill](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Reflections+Bar+and+Grill+17338+International+Blvd+Seattle+WA)
17338 International Blvd — ~0.2 mi, in the DoubleTree
**4.5★ (238 reviews)** · $$ · Open until 11 PM
Lamb burger and clam chowder are the standout orders. A reviewer confirms walking here directly from the Hilton.

### [Sharps RoastHouse](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Sharps+RoastHouse+18427+International+Blvd+SeaTac+WA)
18427 International Blvd — ~0.5 mi (edge of comfortable walking distance)
**4.6★ (8,858 reviews — largest sample in this whole list)** · $$ · Open until midnight
Best food quality within walking range. Ribs and the prime rib dip are frequently recommended; "classy but casual" atmosphere works for a group dinner without booking ahead.

### [RBG Bar and Grill](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=RBG+Bar+and+Grill+18118+International+Blvd+Seattle+WA)
18118 International Blvd — ~0.25 mi
**4.0★ (86 reviews — small sample, mixed)** · $$
Reviews are inconsistent — some praise it, others cite slow service and "meh" food. Fine as a backup, not a first choice.

## Casual (5–10 min drive)

### [Saigon & Siam Palace](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Saigon+and+Siam+Palace+16720+International+Blvd+SeaTac+WA)
16720 International Blvd
**4.5★ (134 reviews)** · $ · Open until 2:30 AM
Vietnamese/Thai/fusion. Repeated "hidden gem" and pho callouts. Useful for late-night options after event sessions run long.
**Cross-reference flag:** this location operated as "Ginger Palace Pan Asian Cuisine" until a recent rebrand — Yelp still lists it under that name at 3.8–3.9★ across 144–356 reviews, notably below the fresh Google rating. Worth a smaller test order before committing a group booking here.

### [Burien Halo Grill](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Burien+Halo+Grill+627+SW+152nd+St+Burien+WA)
627 SW 152nd St, Burien (~10 min drive)
**4.8★ (274 reviews)** · $$
Shrimp tacos and grilled chicken sandwich are the standouts. Closed Tuesdays.

### [Classic Eats](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Classic+Eats+918+SW+152nd+St+Burien+WA)
918 SW 152nd St, Burien (~10 min drive)
**4.6★ (1,851 reviews)** · $$
New Orleans-style — jambalaya, shrimp and grits, biscuits and gravy. Very consistent review record for a place this size. Popular Sunday brunch.

### [Fireside Burien](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Fireside+Burien+15212+6th+Ave+SW+Burien+WA)
15212 6th Ave SW, Burien (~10 min drive)
**4.5★ (378 reviews)** · $$
American comfort food (burgers, steak sandwich), dog-friendly patio. Good casual group option.

### [Outback Steakhouse](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Outback+Steakhouse+16510+Southcenter+Pkwy+Tukwila+WA)
16510 Southcenter Pkwy, Tukwila (~10 min drive)
**4.0★ (3,018 reviews)** · $$
Chain, but reliable — good pick for large groups or attendees with dietary restrictions who want predictable menu items. Note: closed until 3 PM most weekdays (lunch won't work here Mon–Fri).

## Fancy — no drive required

### [Copperleaf Restaurant & Bar](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Copperleaf+Restaurant+18525+36th+Ave+S+SeaTac+WA)
18525 36th Ave S, at Cedarbrook Lodge — ~0.8 mi, the closest option on this whole list
**4.5★ Google (751) · 4.5★ TripAdvisor (724), ranked #2 of 67 restaurants in SeaTac**
This is a genuine fine-dining pick, not just "nicer" — chef Mark Bodinet is a French Laundry alum, and the menu leans farm-to-table Pacific NW (sablefish, seasonal tasting menu). Fireplace dining room, patio. A couple of TripAdvisor reviews note the tasting menu can run bland on an off night; the à la carte menu is the safer bet for a group. This closes the "no fancy option nearby" gap without a drive.

## Nicer (10–15 min drive)

### [JOEY Southcenter](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=JOEY+Southcenter+758+Southcenter+Mall+Tukwila+WA)
758 Southcenter Mall, Tukwila (~10 min drive)
**4.7★ (7,131 reviews — very large sample)** · $$
Upscale-casual chain, full bar, good vegetarian options. Best choice for a polished group dinner without booking a fine-dining reservation.

### [Piacere Trattoria Italiana](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Piacere+Trattoria+Italiana+653+SW+152nd+St+Burien+WA)
653 SW 152nd St, Burien (~10 min drive)
**4.6★ (620 reviews)** · $$
Owner-run, genuinely Italian (not Americanized). Cross-referenced consistent: Yelp 4.4★ (101), TripAdvisor 4.4★, RestaurantGuru 4.5★ (639) — the occasional off-night complaint (cold risotto) looks like an outlier rather than a pattern. Closed Mondays.

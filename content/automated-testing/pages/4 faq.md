# FAQ

**Do I need to install anything to view this site?**
No — it works in any modern browser. Installing it just gives you a home
screen icon and keeps it working without a signal (see
[Installation](/installation)).

**What does "GCA" mean on a session?**
It credits a caller who isn't the session's headliner — someone calling
alongside the main caller(s) without top billing.

**Can I see just my level?**
Yes — the Room Schedule, Dance Schedule, and Caller Schedule pages all
have a level-range slider. Drag either end, or tap a level label directly,
to narrow the range.

**Will this reflect last-minute changes to the schedule?**
It reflects the schedule as of the last update before the event started.
For same-day changes (a room swap, a caller substitution), check the
printed schedule or day-of announcements as well.

**Can I zoom in on an image?**
Yes — tap or click any image on the site to open it full-screen.

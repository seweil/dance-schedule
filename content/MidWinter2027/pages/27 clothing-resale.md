# Clothing Resale

Shop for "experienced" square dance clothes at the festival's resale.

## Selling

Only items pre-submitted on a resale list are accepted; there's no
selling clothes brought to the door.

- **Consignment drop-off:** Thursday from 3:00 PM, or Friday from 9:00 AM.
  Come early.
- Bring items clean, sorted into their categories, with sets put together.
- Volunteers are welcome during consignment and sale hours, even for an
  hour.

[sheilainsalem@hotmail.com](mailto:sheilainsalem@hotmail.com)

## Free outfits for youth

Youth dancers 19 and under can pick out a free outfit on Sunday, 9:00–10:30

# Clothing Resale

Every Mid-Winter there's a chance to sell your "experienced" square dance
clothes, and to shop for new-to-you ones.

## Selling at Mid-Winter 2027

- **Submit your list of items in advance:** mail it by **January 9**, or
  email it by **January 16, 2027**. Clothing that wasn't pre-submitted isn't
  accepted at the door.
- Make sure items are clean and in good repair.
- Bring items sorted into their categories, to make tagging easier, and put
  sets together.
- Come early to consignment: Thursday afternoon (3:00 PM) or Friday morning
  (9:00 AM).
- Volunteer to help during consignment and sale hours, even for just an hour.

The flyer, form, and form instructions are on the
[festival website](https://midwinterfestival.org/2027/resale/).

# Lodging

Tell the hotel you're a Mid-Winter dancer when you book, to get the best
rate.

> **Please note:** for 2027, the hotels will enforce their own early
> check-in and late check-out policies and fees. The festival doesn't control
> hotel policies, so please treat hotel staff with courtesy and respect. See
> the chairwoman's full note on the
> [festival website](https://midwinterfestival.org/2027/dining/).

## RV parking

### Linn County Fairgrounds
3700 Knox Butte Rd SE, Albany · 541-926-4314
67 RV spaces with water and power hookups, near the main buildings:
no-hookup spaces for self-contained units, 30 amp power only, 30 amp with
water, and 50 amp with water.

### Knox Butte RV Park
125 Expo Pkwy NE, Albany · 541-928-9033 or 800-510-6620
Full hookups (electric, sewer, water, cable TV), garbage, and 24-hour
restrooms, showers and laundry. About 2 blocks' walk from the Expo Center.
Internet included with daily or weekly rates.

### The Blue Ox RV Park
4000 Blue Ox Dr SE, Albany · 541-926-2886 or 800-336-2881
I-5 exit 233, about 2 miles from the Expo Center. Recreation room with
kitchen, exercise room, indoor pool, restrooms, laundry, showers, WiFi and
cable, handicap access; dogs and cats welcome.

# Youth

Stop by the **Youth Table in the Plus Hall** (Willamette North, Hall C) for
information about this year's youth activities.

## Friday & Saturday: fun badges

Pick an option for earning a fun badge or dangle at the Youth Table. If it
needs supplies, ask for them there. Once you've completed it, report back to
the Youth Table to get your new fun badge or dangle.

## Saturday: activity bag

Pick up an activity bag at the Youth Table, with some small gifts, a water
bottle, and an activity page. Complete and sign the activity page and drop
it in the box on the Youth Table to be entered in a prize drawing after
7:00 PM.

## Sunday: a free square dance outfit

Youth dancers 19 and under can choose a free square dance outfit from
Resale Clothing, Sunday 9:00–10:30 AM only. The youth dancer must be there
to choose. Outfits can't be given out early, set aside, or picked in
advance.

## Please note

Youth Activities are **not** childcare or babysitting. They're self-run
activities for youth who choose to take part, and a gathering spot for youth
dancers; a supervising adult isn't guaranteed to be there. Youth running in
the halls, or disrupting or endangering other dancers, attendees, or the
facilities, will be taken to their parent or guardian.
